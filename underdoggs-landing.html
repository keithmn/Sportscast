Kenyan sport has world-class athletes, fiercely passionate communities, and stories that deserve to be told with depth and care. For too long, those stories have been ignored, borrowed, or told from the outside — filtered through foreign voices for foreign audiences.

Underdoggs Sports Cast was built to change that. We cover the full width of Kenyan sport — from football to hockey, from the KPL to university pitches — with the quality and passion it has always deserved.

We are an underdog, building something that doesn't exist yet. So were the athletes we cover.
