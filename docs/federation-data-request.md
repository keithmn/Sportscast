# Results data request — draft for federations

A draft to send to each federation's competitions or communications office: FKF, KRU, KBF, KHU, KVF, Athletics Kenya and others. Fill in the [placeholders] first; never send it with them still in. Adjust the competition names for each federation.

---

**Subject:** Publishing [competition] results on The Sportscast — a simple results feed, with full credit to [federation]

Dear [name],

I'm writing from The Sportscast, a Kenyan sports site that puts Kenyan competitions first. We cover [competitions this federation runs, e.g. the KHU Premier League] alongside our match coverage and podcasts.

We'd like the results, fixtures and tables we publish for [competition] to come straight from [federation], so they're correct and on time. At the moment we compile them from public reports, which is slower and can introduce mistakes we then have to correct.

**What we're asking for.** After each round, the results in a shared spreadsheet, or sent to our results desk on WhatsApp. This doesn't need new systems on your side. Each row only needs:

| Date | Home | Home score | Away score | Away | Round | Venue (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18/10/2026 | [Home team] | 2 | 1 | [Away team] | Matchweek 2 | [Ground] |

We'd also welcome the season's fixture list when it's set, and any official decisions that change a table, such as points deductions or awarded games.

**What [federation] gets in return:**
- **Credit everywhere.** Every table and result from your data carries "Data: [federation]" with a link to your site.
- **Speed and accuracy.** Results go up as soon as they're received. Any correction is shown openly on the match page, with what changed and when.
- **Fixtures with venue and broadcaster**, so fans know where to go and where to watch.
- **Coverage and promotion** for [competition] on our site and our social channels: [channels and audience figures — only figures we can show].
- **A named contact on our side:** [name, role, phone, email].

If a formal agreement is needed for publishing [federation]'s data, we're glad to sign one. [Add here if anything should be said about commercial terms.]

Could we have a short call in the next two weeks? [Proposed times.]

Kind regards,
[Name]
[Role], The Sportscast
[Phone] · [Email]

## Notes for whoever sends it (internal — not part of the email)

- **Kenyan Premier League:** results data is the subject of the Milestone Sports Media litigation. Take legal advice before asking FKF or Milestone for a KPL feed or publishing KPL tables, and record the outcome in Competitions → Settings → Rights.
- **Record each reply** in that competition's Rights notes (Admin → Competitions → Settings), and set the status: Asked for permission, then Cleared.
- **Once a sheet arrives,** paste it into Results → choose the competition → *Import from a spreadsheet*. "Check" shows what each row will do before anything changes, and a score that differs from one already published is never overwritten there.
- **Set the credit line** in the competition's Settings, for example "Data: Kenya Hockey Union". It then appears under its tables and results.
